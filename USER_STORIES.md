# Throughline — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Operations manager, claims intake

- As an operations manager, I want each inbound notification classified and clustered on arrival with a routing decision I can see, so that mis-routed post and email stop generating a second manual triage.
- As an operations manager, I want the live conduct clock for every item in my queues by member state, so that I can prioritise against the deadline that actually binds us rather than by age.
- As an operations manager, I want an alert when an automated route starts returning items for rework, so that a quality problem surfaces before it becomes a service breach.
- As an operations manager, I want to see cost per work item by type and route, so that I can argue for or against automating a step with our own numbers.

### Automation centre of excellence lead

- As an automation lead, I want every automated step registered with its owner, its host-market clearances, and its failure behaviour, so that our inventory is auditable rather than tribal knowledge.
- As an automation lead, I want to activate an automation in one member state while it remains blocked in another, so that a single clearance delay does not hold up the whole programme.
- As an automation lead, I want each automation reported against its stated payback period using booked expense, so that I can retire the ones that never paid instead of defending them.
- As an automation lead, I want to insert a cognitive service into an existing legacy straight-through path without double-counting the benefit the legacy path already delivered.

### Finance business partner

- As a finance business partner, I want operations cost metered as attributable or non-attributable to insurance contracts, so that the close does not require a manual reallocation exercise.
- As a finance business partner, I want automation benefit expressed in expense-ratio basis points by line and market, so that I can reconcile the programme's claims to the reported combined ratio.
- As a finance business partner, I want released capacity to have a recorded disposition, so that a saving is either a reduced cost or a documented absorption rather than an assertion.

### Conduct and compliance officer

- As a compliance officer for a host market, I want to withhold or withdraw clearance for a specific automated step in my market, so that local conduct rules are enforced by configuration rather than by memo.
- As a compliance officer, I want evidence that renewal workflows are blind to inferred price-sensitivity signals, so that I can answer a differential-pricing review with a system record.
- As a compliance officer, I want every automated pay-out to show its human approver and authority limit, so that settlement control is demonstrable to internal audit.
- As a compliance officer, I want the conversational assistant's escalation and answer-quality rates by market, so that I can judge whether customers are being served or deflected.

### Complaints and vulnerable-customer handler

- As a complaints handler, I want the full route history of the complained-of item, including every automated decision and its policy version, so that I can respond substantively within the ombudsman timetable.
- As a complaints handler, I want items flagged as vulnerable-customer or hardship cases pulled out of automated routing into a named human queue, so that the people least able to navigate automation do not receive it.
